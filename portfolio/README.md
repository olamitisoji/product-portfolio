## Echo

I designed Echo for researchers who want the back-and-forth of an interview without scheduling a call. Participants answer on WhatsApp by voice note or text, and Echo uses the researcher’s guide to decide when to ask a follow-up. In our team’s experience, research turnaround fell from roughly two weeks to under three days. We completed an interview on Meta’s WhatsApp test number with consent, voice transcription, follow-up questions, pause and resume.

## Sortward

I built a trailer heatmap during my Amazon internship so shift leads could see where an incoming load was headed. Door suggestions went into pilot. In Sortward, my independent simulator, you can see which of the facility’s 600 chutes a fictional load will feed and compare its door options. On the sort floor, ask the AI analyst where one more staffed position would help most. You can inspect the records it cites and try the move yourself.
