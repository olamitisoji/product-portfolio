## Echo

While researching healthcare administration, my team heard from people who were happy to send voice notes but couldn’t find time for a call. I designed a WhatsApp interviewer that lets researchers ask follow-up questions while participants respond on their own schedule. In our team’s experience, research turnaround fell from roughly two weeks to under three days. We completed an interview on Meta’s WhatsApp test number with consent, voice transcription, follow-up questions, pause and resume.

## Dockflow

An inbound operator can have an urgent trailer in the queue and an empty door that can’t take its freight. I built a simulator that shows why an assignment is feasible or blocked, and gives the operator the final decision. You can change the queue order, approve an assignment, and undo it using fictional warehouse data.
